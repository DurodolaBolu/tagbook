What Tagbook does

Built with laundry owners, for the front desk. Simple enough for anyone in the shop to use on their phone.

Log clothes in secondsShirts, senators, agbada, bedsheets, duvets. Add the colour, number of pieces and a photo of any stain.
Bills from your own price listTagbook adds up the bill from your prices, including express and wait-and-get.
Send on WhatsAppSend the customer the list of their clothes at drop-off and the bill when they're ready.
Customer tracking linkCustomers can check on their clothes any time from a link, without calling the shop.
Know what is dueSee which orders are late, due today and due tomorrow, at a glance.
Payments and debtsRecord full or part payments and see who still owes, so no money is forgotten.
Staff loginsEach staff member has their own login. Choose who can see money and who only handles clothes.
Reports and receiptsMoney in and spending by day, week, month and year. Print receipts on a thermal printer.

See it on a phone

Tagbook works in the phone's browser and installs like an app on Android and iPhone.
