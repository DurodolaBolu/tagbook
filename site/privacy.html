Tagbook

Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

In short: Tagbook keeps a laundry shop's records of customers, clothes and payments so the shop can run its business. We do not sell anyone's information, we do not show ads, and we do not track people for marketing.

Who we are

Tagbook is run by Ordiventa (Boluwatife Durodola), based in Akure, Ondo State, Nigeria. You can reach us on WhatsApp at +234 812 075 6325.

Who this policy covers

  • Shops: the owners and staff who log in to Tagbook.
  • Shops' customers: the people whose clothes a shop records in Tagbook. Customers do not log in.

For customers' details, the shop decides what to record and is responsible for it. We store and handle those details for the shop, only to provide Tagbook. For owner and staff accounts, we are responsible.

What we collect

  • Owners and staff: name, phone number, role, and your PIN, which is stored only in a scrambled form that we cannot read. If you turn on notifications, a code for your phone so we can send them.
  • The shop: shop name, branches, price list, bank details the shop chooses to show on bills, and Tagbook subscription payments. Payments are made through Paystack; we never see card numbers.
  • Shops' customers: name, phone number, tag mark, how the shop greets them, the clothes they drop off, bills and payments.
  • Login safety: when someone tries to log in, we record the phone number and internet address used, so we can stop people guessing PINs.

Why we use it

  • To provide Tagbook to the shop: recording clothes, preparing bills, tracking payments and sending messages the shop chooses to send.
  • To keep accounts safe, for example by pausing a login after too many wrong PINs.
  • To bill the shop for Tagbook and give referral rewards.
  • To meet our duties under Nigerian law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Who can see it

  • The shop owner sees all of the shop's records.
  • Staff see only what they need to do their work. They never see customers' phone numbers, bills or payments, and they see the price list only if the owner allows it.
  • Other shops can never see your shop's records.
  • Ordiventa sees summary counts for each shop (for example, how many orders). We open a shop's records only to fix a problem the shop asks us to fix, or when the law requires it.
  • Service providers who run parts of Tagbook for us: Supabase (database and logins), Cloudflare (website), Paystack (payments) and YouTube (help videos). They may only use the information to provide their service.

Bills and lists sent to customers go from the shop's own WhatsApp, not from Tagbook.

Where it is kept and how it is protected

Information is stored on servers run by Supabase and Cloudflare, which may be outside Nigeria. It travels encrypted, each shop's records are kept apart by rules in the database, and logins are paused after repeated wrong PINs.

How long we keep it

We keep a shop's records while the shop uses Tagbook. If a shop closes its account, we delete its records within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep something longer. Login safety records are kept only as long as needed to stop PIN guessing.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, or object to how it is used. Customers of a shop should ask the shop first; you can also contact us and we will work with the shop. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

If something goes wrong

If we learn that information has been exposed, we will tell the affected shops and, where the law requires, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell shops in the app when the change matters to them.
